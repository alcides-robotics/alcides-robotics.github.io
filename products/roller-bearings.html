Roller bearings

Precision bearings that reduce friction and wear

Roller bearings

ALCiDES roller bearings distribute loads over small contact surfaces to reduce friction and wear, so moving parts in machines and systems run smoothly. Designs cover ball and roller types for radial and axial loads across automotive, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, wind energy, and more. Custom solutions are available according to your samples or drawings.

Product types

Deep groove ball bearings

Universally applicable bearings for high speeds and loads — for example in electric motors, machine tools, and fans. Often maintenance-friendly and resistant to moisture and dirt.

Cylindrical roller bearings

High load-carrying capacity with cylindrical rollers, available with or without cage. Suited to heavy radial loads in industrial drives and machinery.

Spherical roller bearings

Two-row designs that compensate for misalignment and shaft deflection during rotation, keeping operation stable under demanding conditions.

Tapered roller bearings

Conical rollers and raceways for combined radial and one-sided axial loads. Inner and outer rings can be mounted independently.

Crossed roller bearings

X-arranged cylindrical rollers with spacers for multi-directional loads and high rigidity — ideal for machine tools, robots, and rotary tables.

Axial roller bearings

Designed for high axial forces on one or both sides. Housing washers can be mounted independently and must not take radial load.

Spindle bearings

Precision angular contact ball bearings for high speed and accuracy in the precision industry, available in various tolerance classes.

Thin-section bearings

Compact thin-ring designs that save space while handling high radial loads — useful in machine tools, textile machinery, and confined installations.

Mast rollers & needle bearings

Mast rollers with reinforced outer rings for rail-guided loads, plus needle bearings for limited-space applications in open or sealed versions.

Rolling elements & custom-made

Balls, cylindrical rollers, and needle rollers in the required grades and materials, plus custom ring-shaped, cast, or forged parts manufactured to your specifications.
