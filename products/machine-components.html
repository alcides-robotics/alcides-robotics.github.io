Machine components

ALCiDES manufactures machine components — forged and cast parts — according to your requirements and material specifications in small and large series. From automotive and vehicle accessories to industrial spare parts, we support you from specification through delivery.

Typical components

Vehicle & automotive parts

Brake discs and cylinders, clutches, rims, water pumps, switches, fittings, pipes and hoses, as well as compact and wheel bearings and related accessories.

Drive & electrical components

Components for electric motors and related assemblies, including plastic parts and electronic components manufactured to your drawings.

Material handling spare parts

Spare parts for material handling equipment and other industrial machinery, produced in the volumes you need.

Custom forged & cast parts

Individual material grades and geometries according to customer samples or drawings — for prototypes through series production.
